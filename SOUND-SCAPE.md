# Soundscape map

All sounds are synthesized at runtime from oscillators and generated noise; no recordings, network requests, paid voices or external audio libraries are used. Voice remains the optional device speech preview. Sound and volume are available on the title screen, every cinematic and the room settings. The text and visual clues remain authoritative.

- Ceremony and shop (episodes 1–2): distant bell at the opening, then sheltered shop and canal water. The crystal alignment gives a soft success cue.
- Canal, workshop, archive and artisan/tower (3–7): water, timber, paper, crystal and distant bell gestures reflect the investigation and Kai's tower observations.
- Barge, warehouse and passage (8–13): water and hull give way to quieter wood and array resonance, then water in stone. The beginning of the escape receives a low warning.
- Chamber, search, ascent and vigil (14–17): water and crystal soften beneath narration; selected revelations and the search receive a brief motif.
- Summit, revelation and memory (18–20): more open air at the summit, then crystal resonance and water for the Order's account and the city's memory.
- Network, records, restoration and initiation (21–24): water, paper and crystal gestures return as the city's channels and knowledge are restored; a restrained motif marks the closing revelation.

Environmental events occur at long irregular intervals; object responses are bounded and rate-limited. Water, wood and array layers pan and attenuate relative to the player, without conveying exclusive puzzle information. The ambient bed is deliberately subdued. The master mix ducks while device speech plays, suspends on backgrounding, and releases its sources on mute. Actual tonal balance, device autoplay behavior and mobile performance require listening and physical-device review.
